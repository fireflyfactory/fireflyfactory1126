welcome

This site is maintained by a twenty-year-old autist with no direction in life. Firefly Factory as a name originates from a poem I wrote in the summer of 2022; the sight of driving up I-95 in the dark, seeing the single-file rows of headlights and streetlamps and constellations emerging from the dark like comets orbiting a highway of stars.
